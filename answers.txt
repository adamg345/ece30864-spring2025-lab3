    - Helps teams work together, and effective teamwork gets more done.
    - Helps hold people accountable to particular roles and responsibilities.
Cons:
    - Agile can at times make things feel overcomplicated.
    - Could possibly slow things down at times.

b)
    Scrum Masters are focused on the scrum component of the project, whereas the product owner is focused on the entire software development process as a whole.
    
c)
    - Sprint Planning Meeting:
        - Stories from the backlog are moved to the sprint.
        - It's determine what's to be completed in the sprint.

    -Daily Scrum Meeting:
        - Short meeting that happens every day.
        - Workers give a quick update on what's been completed and what they plan to complete for the day.

    - Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
        - Occurs at end of sprint.
        - Any work that's still incomplete is backlogged.
